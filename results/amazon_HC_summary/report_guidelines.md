The final report should present a clear and self-contained account of your project. It must include the following components:

Background and Motivation. Introduce the problem setting, explain why it is interesting or important, and clearly state the research question or objective of the project.

Methodology. Describe the models, algorithms, data, and experimental setup used in your work. Clearly justify your design choices and assumptions.

Contributions. Clearly state your main contributions. These may include theoretical insights, algorithmic developments, empirical findings, or a novel application or analysis.

Conclusions and Future Work. Summarize the main findings and discuss their implications. Outline possible directions for future research or extensions of your work.

Project Roadmap (Appendix). Include a brief week-by-week roadmap of the project’s progress, documenting major milestones, iterations, and changes in direction.

You are expected to incorporate and address the feedback you received on the final presentation, either by revising the relevant sections or explicitly clarifying points that were raised.
